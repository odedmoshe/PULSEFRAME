## 0. How to read this report

**Evidence tags**
- **[V]** = confirmed in a search result during this session (source URL listed in that section).
- **[K]** = from my prior knowledge of the product; not re-confirmed this session. Treat as "probably right, check before quoting".
- **unverified** = I looked and could not confirm, or sources conflict.

**Limits of this research**
- Direct page fetches to `www.capcut.com` and `pixflow.net` were blocked by this session's network allowlist. CapCut's own pages were therefore read only through search-result summaries. If CapCut's official help pages matter, `www.capcut.com` has to be allowed in the session's network settings.
- Much of the 2026 coverage is vendor blogs and affiliate reviews. I preferred vendor release notes and trade press where they existed.
- Exact names of in-app effect and transition categories differ by platform, region and version. No official, complete list of CapCut effect names was reachable.

**Product abbreviations**

## 4. Speed

### CapCut "Curve" presets (Speed > Curve)
Six presets plus Custom [V]. Descriptions come from third-party guides; the exact curve shapes should be checked in the app.

| Preset | Behaviour | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Montage | Sources disagree: either "speeds up, then drops hard into slow motion" or "alternates fast and slow rhythmically" | Multi-moment action clips |
| Hero | Eases down into slow motion around a middle moment, then returns to speed (fast, slow, fast) | One dramatic beat: a jump, a reveal |
| Bullet | Near-instant drop to deep slow motion, then back up ("bullet time") | Impact moments |
| Jump Cut | Alternating fast and slow steps that feel like jump cuts | Synced to beats |
| Flash In | Starts fast, eases to normal | Entering a scene |
| Flash Out | Starts normal, ramps fast at the end | Leaving a scene, feeding a transition |
| Custom | User drags 5 or more control points on a speed graph (roughly 0.1x–10x) [K] | Anything |

Separate from Curve: CapCut **Style > Velocity** presets (for example "Flash", "Blur Shake", "Retro Zoom") combine a speed change with a visual effect [V].

## B. Twenty original ideas not found in the surveyed products

Honesty note: "not found" means it did not turn up in this survey or my prior knowledge of these products. I did not run a dedicated novelty search for each idea, so treat novelty as **unverified**. Adjacent prior art outside the surveyed set is named where I know it.

1. **Prosody typography.** Caption word size, weight and letter spacing follow each word's measured loudness and pitch, so shouted words grow and whispers shrink, and words held longer than expected stretch horizontally. Build: per-word RMS and pitch from the audio, word duration against a syllable-count estimate, mapped to text style parameters. (Research prototypes of speech-driven typography exist; surveyed products use keyword emphasis instead.)
2. **Breath-aware J/L cuts.** Cuts between sentences are placed on the inhale, and the next line's audio leads the picture by a few frames automatically. Build: detect breaths as low-energy broadband noise between transcript words; offset the audio and video edit points.
3. **Pacing heatmap and lint.** A strip above the timeline shows cuts per minute, words per second, loudness and on-screen motion, with warnings such as "12 s with no visual change" or "captions over 20 characters per second". Build: all inputs already exist (edit points, transcript timing, loudness, frame-difference energy).
4. **Elastic music.** The music is micro-stretched between beats so its beats land on your existing cuts, instead of moving cuts to the beat. Build: beat grid plus a piecewise time-stretch map constrained to a few percent per bar, pitch-preserving stretch. (Resolve and Premiere retime music to a target length, not to cut points.)
5. **Word-anchored keyframes.** Keyframes attach to transcript words rather than timestamps, so a zoom "on the word 'free'" survives any re-edit or speed change. Build: keyframe time stored as a word reference plus offset, resolved through the clip's time map. (Descript ties scenes to the script; I did not find per-property keyframes anchored to words.)
6. **Stress pop.** The subject scales forward slightly (with depth-based parallax if a depth map exists) on stressed syllables. Build: stress = local loudness and pitch peaks per word; drive matte-layer scale or depth displacement in the shader.
7. **Captions as light.** Caption text acts as an emissive light source that tints and casts a soft glow onto the scene behind it. Build: blur the text layer into a light buffer and add it to the scene weighted by a depth or normal map; a cheaper version uses screen-space falloff only.
8. **Beat-phase modulation for every parameter.** Any effect parameter can be bound to bar/beat phase (for example one kaleidoscope rotation per bar, strobe at eighth notes) rather than only triggering on beats. Build: expose `beatPhase`, `barPhase` and subdivision uniforms from the beat grid to all shaders. (Common in VJ tools such as Resolume; not found in the surveyed editors.)
9. **Stem-reactive effects.** Drums, bass and vocals drive different parameters: kick to zoom punch, hi-hat to grain flicker, vocals to glow. Build: stem separation as an offline job, per-stem envelopes as uniforms.
10. **Match-cut finder.** The editor proposes pairs of frames across clips with similar composition and auto-aligns scale and position for a match cut. Build: downscaled edge/shape descriptors or image embeddings per sampled frame, nearest-neighbour search, similarity-transform fit. (Netflix published research on this; not found in the surveyed editors. CapCut's "match cut" material is generative.)
11. **Depth-sliced transitions.** The incoming clip appears front-to-back by depth layer, or the outgoing clip dissolves plane by plane. Build: depth map per clip; transition shader thresholds depth against progress.
12. **Visual ducking.** Background effect intensity, saturation or blur automatically drops while someone is speaking and blooms during music-only passages. Build: speech-activity envelope (already needed for audio ducking) mapped to effect amount.
13. **Caption physics.** Words drop in with simple physics and collide with the subject's silhouette, piling on shoulders or sliding off. Build: 2D rigid-body simulation in a worker using the matte's contour as a collider; deterministic seed so export matches preview.
14. **Voice aura.** A glow around the speaker's outline whose radius and colour follow voice loudness and pitch. Build: distance field from the matte edge; radius and hue uniforms from the audio envelope. (Static aura effects and audio visualisers exist separately; I did not find them combined on the subject outline.)
15. **Dramatic-pause emphasis.** Instead of deleting a pause, the editor offers to keep it and add a slow push-in, slight desaturation and a music dip. Build: silences from the transcript, a generated keyframe bundle the user accepts or rejects.
16. **Section-aware looks.** The colour look changes with the song's structure (verse, chorus, drop), cross-fading LUTs at section boundaries. Build: section detection from a self-similarity matrix of audio features; LUT blend uniform keyed to sections.
17. **Seamless loop finder.** Finds in/out points where the last frame and audio phase match the first, for Shorts that loop invisibly. Build: frame-similarity search constrained to beat-aligned candidates plus a short cross-fade. (Exists in some GIF tools; not found in the surveyed editors.)
18. **Target energy curve.** The user draws the intended energy over time; the editor compares it with measured energy (cut rate, loudness, motion) and suggests where to tighten, add a punch-in or drop the music. Build: the heatmap data from idea 3 plus a difference view and suggestion rules.
19. **Stutter-on-consonant.** Micro freeze or frame-repeat effects triggered by plosive consonants (p, t, k, b) in the transcript for rhythmic speech edits. Build: phoneme-ish timing from word text and audio transients; frame-hold effect for 2–4 frames.
20. **Transcript-scoped effects.** An effect's range is defined as "from this word to that word" or "every time this word is said", and follows the words through edits. Build: effect ranges stored as word references, the same mechanism as idea 5, plus a text search across the transcript.

**Considered and dropped because something similar exists**
- Auto zoom on punchlines: auto-zoom exists in OpusClip/Captions-class tools.
- Speed-up of pauses: Descript word-gap shortening and silence removers.
- Quantise all cuts to the beat with a strength setting: close to auto beat-sync templates (CapCut, VN BeatsClip).
- Auto emoji and auto B-roll from transcript: Opus, Captions.
- Chaptered progress bar from transcript: progress bars and chapters exist separately in Veed/Kapwing/Descript.
- Hook A/B variants: Edits has variant testing.
- Multi-plane depth captions: an extension of text-behind-subject.
- Faux datamosh: available as effects/plugins.
- Edit-rhythm copy from a reference video: I could not rule out an existing CapCut feature.
- Caption placement that avoids faces and platform UI: partially covered by safe zones and smart placement in caption tools.

### Practical reading for a 4 GB GPU
- **Comfortable:** BiRefNet (or lite), RVM, SAM 2.1 tiny/small, Depth Anything V2 Small, Video Depth Anything Small (likely), Real-ESRGAN small models with tiling, RIFE, FILM at 720p, LaMa, Wav2Lip, shader relighting from depth.
- **Possible with care (short clips, low resolution, fp16):** MatAnyone, MuseTalk, ProPainter at about 480p, IC-Light V1 on stills.
- **Not plausible:** SeedVR2, FlashVSR (unverified), GIMM-VFI, LatentSync, DiffuEraser, Wan VACE, MiniMax-Remover, InfiniteTalk, Light-A-Video, IC-Light V2.
- **Licence traps for a product:** RMBG-2.0 and RMBG-1.4 (non-commercial), Depth Anything V2 Base/Large and Video Depth Anything Large (CC BY-NC), MatAnyone and ProPainter (S-Lab non-commercial), Wav2Lip (non-commercial), RVM (GPL-3.0), IC-Light V2 (non-commercial).
- **Commercial-friendly set:** BiRefNet, SAM 2.1, Depth Anything V2 Small, Video Depth Anything Small, Real-ESRGAN, RIFE, FILM, LaMa, MuseTalk, SeedVR2 (if hardware allows).
